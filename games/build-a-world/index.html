How to play

Choose a block from the colorful toolbox. Then tap anywhere in your world to place it. Drag to build walls, floors, and fun shapes faster.

Tap 🎁 Surprise Me! to see a random building, or 🔄 Rebuild to clear every block. You can use ↶ Undo to bring your last creation back. Change day to night and save your picture too!
