# Pumpkin Face Lab 🎃

A touch-friendly pumpkin-carving game for making silly and spooky faces.

## Play

- Pick a cutter from **Eyes**, **Noses**, **Mouths**, or **Extras**, then tap the pumpkin to stamp a glowing shape.
- Choose **Jack-o'-lantern** for lighted cutouts or **Black carving** for unlit, black cutouts. Changing styles preserves the current face.
- Use **Draw** to carve custom lines with a finger, or **Move** to select and drag a stamped piece.
- Press **↶ Left** or **↷ Right** to tilt the selected stamped piece by 15 degrees. The most recently stamped shape is selected automatically.
- **Undo**, **Blank**, and **Surprise me!** help try more ideas.
- **Save pumpkin** adds the current face to **My Pumpkin Patch**, where the latest nine creations can be reopened.

Saved pumpkins and the in-progress face use browser-local storage. They are not synchronized with the earlier standalone Pumpkin Face Lab site or other devices.

The game is fully self-contained and works offline once its page is loaded. The only Arcade integrations are the shared return-to-Arcade stylesheet and analytics scripts.
