# ❄️ Snowball Playground

Pack snowballs and throw them at a brick wall, targets, or a snowman.

- Hold **Hold to pack a snowball** to grow it, then release.
- Tap the playground to throw, or drag and release to aim.
- Start with Snowman, or choose Target practice or Brick wall. Fresh snow resets the scene and counters.
- Unlimited snow, no account, and no saved game state.

Uses the shared Arcade return button and aggregate analytics. Canvas graphics are drawn locally; no external assets or microphone access are required.
