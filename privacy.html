Privacy & analytics

Welcome to our little family arcade! We want everyone to enjoy the games without needing an account.

Optional website statistics

We'd love to understand how people use our arcade — which game pages are popular, how many visits we get, and general locations and device types.

If you choose to allow statistics, we use Google Analytics to help us improve the website. Google Analytics may use cookies and other identifiers.

We don't send players' names, email addresses, game answers, or individual gameplay details to Analytics. We don't use Analytics for advertising or personalized ads.
