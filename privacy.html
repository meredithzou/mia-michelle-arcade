Privacy

Welcome to our little family arcade! Our games are free to play, and you don't need an account.

Simple website statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which game pages are popular, general visitor locations and device types, and how our website performs. This helps us make the arcade better.

Cloudflare Web Analytics doesn't use tracking cookies or device fingerprinting. Your browser sends basic page and performance information to Cloudflare, but we don't track individual players or use these statistics for advertising.

We don't send player names, email addresses, game answers, or scores to this analytics service. Learn about Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Some games remember your settings on this device using browser storage. These preferences are not sent to our analytics service.

Optional microphone input

Piano Note Finder and Play That Note! offer optional microphone input. Permission is requested only when you tap Listen or turn on the microphone. Sound is analyzed live on your device to estimate a single note. These games do not record, save, or upload audio, and do not send detected notes or microphone levels to analytics. Tap Stop, turn off the microphone, or leave the page to stop microphone access.
