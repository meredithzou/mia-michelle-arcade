Privacy & analytics

Mia and Michelle's Arcade is a small family game website. The games are available whether or not you choose to allow optional website statistics.

Current status

Analytics is currently disabled.

Analytics is off by default. Enabling it requires a separate website update and privacy review.

What optional statistics would involve

If site analytics is enabled in the future, we plan to use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate page visits, popular game pages, broad geographic regions, and device categories. GA4 may use first-party cookies and pseudonymous identifiers when a visitor opts in. These are not guaranteed to be anonymous.

We do not intend to send players' names, contact details, game answers, or individual gameplay events. We do not connect Google Ads or enable advertising personalization. Detailed game activity is not part of this phase.

Your choice

Analytics will not load before you opt in. You can decline without losing access to any game. If enabled later, your choice will be saved in this browser, and you can change it on this page. Withdrawing consent stops future analytics and attempts to clear GA4 cookies on this website; it does not retroactively erase previously collected reports.

Privacy and children's use

Children may play these games. Website statistics remain disabled until the site owner has reviewed applicable child-privacy obligations and the suitability of the analytics service. A website choice button is not a substitute for verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.
